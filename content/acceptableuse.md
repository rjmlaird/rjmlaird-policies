## Overview

This policy sets out the rules for using interactive tools, submitting content, accessing client areas or communities, and using APIs or data services provided by **{{site.controllerName}}** through {{site.siteUrl | link}} and related services.

It applies in addition to the [terms of use](/termsofuse/). If a service has its own terms, those apply too. Not every service described here is available at every time.

## Using the services responsibly

You must use the services lawfully and with respect for other people. You must not:

- Break any law or regulation, or encourage others to do so
- Harass, threaten, abuse, discriminate against or impersonate any person
- Upload or share material that is unlawful, defamatory, hateful, sexually explicit, or that exploits or endangers children
- Infringe anyone’s intellectual-property, privacy or other rights
- Share another person’s personal information without a lawful reason and permission
- Spread misleading, deceptive or fraudulent material, or use the services for scams, spam or unsolicited marketing
- Attempt to gain unauthorised access to accounts, data, systems or networks, or probe or test for weaknesses except as allowed by the [security notice](/security/)
- Introduce malware or other harmful code, or disrupt, overload or damage the services
- Bypass or interfere with security, rate limits, access controls or technical restrictions
- Use automated tools, scrapers or bots except where the service expressly allows them
- Use the services, or content from them, to train or develop an AI or machine-learning system unless expressly permitted in writing

## Content you submit

If you submit content, for example a story, comment, form entry, file or message:

- You must own it or have permission to share it, and it must follow this policy
- You are responsible for what you submit, and for removing personal or confidential information that should not be included
- Do not include special-category or sensitive personal information unless the service specifically asks for it and explains why

## Moderation and removal

I may review, edit, decline or remove submitted content, and may limit or end access, where I reasonably believe this policy has been broken, where there is a legal, safety, security or reputational risk, or where content is not appropriate for the service.

Where reasonable and safe, I will tell you what has been removed and why. I may report unlawful activity to the relevant authorities, and I may need to share information to protect people or to comply with the law.

## Accounts and client areas

If you have an account or access to a client area, you are responsible for keeping your details secure and for activity under your account. Do not share your access with others unless this has been agreed. Tell me promptly if you think your access has been compromised. I may suspend access where needed to protect security or other users.

## APIs and data services

Where I provide an API, dataset or data service, you must also:

- Use it only for the purposes allowed in its documentation or licence
- Stay within published rate limits and usage allowances: {{site.commercial.apiLimits}}
- Keep any access keys secret, and not share, sell or sub-license access unless permitted
- Credit sources and licences as the documentation requires
- Not present data as endorsed, certified or verified by me unless it has been
- Check that data is suitable for your purpose before relying on it, as it is provided without any warranty beyond that stated in its documentation

I may change, limit or withdraw an API or dataset, or change its limits, and will give notice where reasonably practicable.

## Reporting a problem

To report content or behaviour that breaks this policy, email {{site.contactEmail | mailto}}. To report a suspected security vulnerability, follow the [security notice](/security/). If a child or adult is at risk, see the [safeguarding policy](/safeguarding/), and call 999 in an emergency.

## Consequences

If this policy is broken, I may take proportionate action, including a warning, removal of content, suspension or ending of access, and, where required, reporting to the authorities. Actions taken do not affect any other rights I have.
