## Overview

This policy explains when **{{site.controllerName}}** takes, records, uses and shares photographs, video and audio, and how consent works.

It applies to events, talks, workshops, tutoring and mentoring sessions, online meetings, and images collected for this website, social media and other publicity. It applies with the [[Privacy notice]], the [[Data protection policy]] and the [[Safeguarding policy]].

A photograph, video or voice recording of an identifiable person is personal data. I handle it with care and only where I have a lawful basis.

## When I take or record media

- **Events and public talks:** I, or someone I engage, may take photographs and video to report on the event and promote future ones. Where the event is mine, I will tell you before you arrive or at registration, and again at the venue
- **Online sessions:** sessions are not recorded unless this has been agreed in advance. If a recording is made, I will say when it starts
- **Tutoring and mentoring:** I do not photograph or record learners unless the learner, or the parent or carer for a learner under 18, has agreed in advance and in writing
- **Marketing and portfolio:** I will ask before I use images of identifiable clients or participants in case studies, testimonials or promotional material

## Consent

Where I rely on consent:

- It will be freely given, specific, informed and clear. You will know what is being taken, where it may appear and for how long
- You can say yes to some uses and no to others, for example internal use but not social media
- Saying no will not affect the service you receive
- You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting {{site.privacyEmail | mailto}}. I will stop using the media from then on and remove it from sites and channels I control, though I cannot recall printed material already distributed or content others have shared

At events where it is not practical to ask everyone individually, I may rely on legitimate interests for general crowd and venue photographs, with clear signs, an easy way to opt out, and no close-up identifiable images of someone who has objected. I will not use this basis for children or for any image I want to use in a way you would not reasonably expect.

## Children and young people

Extra care applies to anyone under 18.

- I need written consent from a parent or carer before I take or use images or recordings of a child, and I take into account the child’s own wishes
- I do not publish a child’s full name alongside their image, and I do not publish a home address, school or location details that could identify where they are
- I do not take images in changing areas, toilets or other private spaces, or in any way that could cause embarrassment or distress
- I do not use images of children for anything other than the stated purpose
- Parents and carers should not photograph or record other people’s children at events or online sessions unless the organiser has said it is allowed

If I have a safeguarding concern about an image or recording, I will follow the [[Safeguarding policy]].

## Using and sharing media

I may use agreed media on this website, on social media, in newsletters, reports and presentations, and with partners or funders where you were told. I will:

- Use images fairly and respectfully, and not in a misleading way
- Credit photographers where required
- Store media securely, in line with the [[Information security policy]]
- Keep media only as long as needed for the purpose, as set out in the [[Data retention policy]]

I do not sell images of identifiable people, and I do not allow anyone to use them to train AI or facial-recognition systems. I do not use AI tools to alter images of identifiable people in a way that misrepresents them. See the [[AI use policy]].

## Third-party photographers

Anyone I engage to photograph or film an event for me must follow this policy, and the arrangements for how they store and use images will be agreed in writing. Journalists and members of the public may take photographs in public places, and their use of images is for them to look after.

## Attending as a member of the public

If you attend one of my events, you can:

- Ask the organiser, at registration or on the day, not to be photographed, and I will respect that as far as is reasonable at a public event
- Ask me to remove an image you appear in from places I control
- Tell me if you are concerned about how an image has been used

## Your rights

Your rights over personal data, including access, deletion and objection, apply to images and recordings. See the [[Data protection policy]]. To make a request, contact {{site.privacyEmail | mailto}}. If you are not satisfied, you can use the [[Complaints policy]], or complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

## Copyright and use by others

I keep the copyright in images and recordings I create unless an agreement says otherwise. You must not copy, reproduce, publish or share media from my events or sessions for commercial purposes without permission. Recording of online sessions by attendees is not allowed unless I have agreed.

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The current version is published on this page with the date it was last updated.
