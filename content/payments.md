## Overview

This policy explains how fees, invoices, deposits, card payments, late payments and refunds work for services provided by **{{site.controllerName}}**.

It applies with the [terms of service](/termsofservice/) and the [booking and cancellation policy](/cancellation/). It does not limit your statutory rights.

## Fees

Fees are set out in the proposal, quote or booking page for the service. Fees are in pounds sterling unless stated otherwise. {{site.commercial.vatStatement}}

I will tell you about any expenses, travel or third-party costs before they are incurred, and I will not charge for them unless they are agreed in the proposal or you have approved them.

## How to pay

You can pay:

- Online by card through the booking portal or a payment link, using a third-party payment provider (currently Stripe)

## Invoices

Invoices are issued once the work is agreed, delivered or at the milestones set out in the proposal, and are payable within {{site.commercial.invoiceTermsDays}} days of the invoice date unless the proposal says otherwise.

Please tell me promptly, and before the due date, if you dispute any part of an invoice. You should pay any part that is not in dispute by the due date, and I will work with you in good faith to resolve the rest.

## Late payment

If an invoice is not paid by the due date, I may send a reminder and may pause work or further bookings until the account is up to date.

If you are a business client, I may charge statutory interest and fixed-sum compensation for late payment under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998. If you are a consumer, I will not charge more than the law allows, and I will try to agree a sensible payment arrangement with you first.

## Failed or disputed payments

If a payment fails or is declined, I will tell you and give you a reasonable chance to pay in another way before a booking is released. If you raise a dispute or chargeback with your card provider, please contact me first so that I can try to put things right, and please give me the information needed to respond.

## Refunds

Refunds depend on the circumstances:

- **You cancel in time:** you will be refunded as set out in the [booking and cancellation policy](/cancellation/).
- **I cancel:** you will receive a full refund of any fee paid for the session or event that did not take place, unless you choose to rearrange.
- **Consumer right to cancel:** you will be refunded as set out in the [consumer rights section](/cancellation/#your-statutory-rights-as-a-consumer) of the cancellation policy.
- **A problem with the service:** if a service is not carried out with reasonable care and skill, you may be entitled to have it repeated, or to a price reduction or refund. Please contact me and I will consider it fairly.

I will not generally refund services that have been properly provided, work that has already been delivered, or fees for sessions missed or cancelled late, except as set out in this policy or required by law.

### How to ask for a refund

Email {{site.contactEmail | mailto}} with your name, the booking or invoice reference and the reason. I will respond within {{site.commercial.refundDecisionDays}} business days.

### How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. I aim to send approved refunds within {{site.commercial.refundPaymentDays}} business days, although your bank or card provider may take longer to show the money in your account.

## Credits

If a booking is moved to a credit instead of a refund, I will confirm the credit amount and how long it can be used for in writing.

## Tax and records

I keep invoices and payment records as required by tax, accounting and legal obligations, as described in the [privacy notice](/privacy/).

## Questions and complaints

If you have a question about a payment, contact {{site.contactEmail | mailto}}. If you are unhappy with how a payment or refund was handled, see the [complaints policy](/complaints/).

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The version that applies to a booking or invoice is the one in force when the booking or agreement was made.
