## Overview

They apply with the [Conditions of sale](/conditionsofsale/), the [Payment policy](/payments/) and the [Acceptable use policy](/acceptableuse/). If the plan page says something different about a particular plan, the plan page applies for that plan.

Nothing in these terms limits the rights you have as a consumer under the law.

## What you get

Each plan has a name, a description of what is included, a price and a billing period (for example monthly or yearly). These are shown on the plan page and in your confirmation before you pay. I may improve a plan over time. If I make a change that reduces what you receive in a significant way, I will tell you in advance and you may cancel.

## Signing up

To subscribe, you must be 18 or over, or have a parent or carer’s agreement, and you must give accurate details. A subscription starts when I confirm it and your payment succeeds.

Before you pay, the checkout will tell you:

- The price, what it includes and how often you will be charged
- When the plan renews, and for how long the minimum term is, if there is one
- How to cancel
- Whether there is a free or discounted trial, what happens at the end of it, and the price you will pay afterwards

## Free and discounted trials

If a plan starts with a free or discounted trial, I will say how long it lasts and the full price that will be charged when it ends. Unless you cancel before the end of the trial, the subscription will continue and you will be charged the full price. I will remind you before the trial ends.

## Billing and renewal

- Your payment method will be charged at the start of each billing period, until you cancel
- Payments are taken through a third-party payment provider. See the [Payment policy](/payments/)
- Prices include or exclude VAT as shown at checkout. {{site.commercial.vatStatement}}
- I will email you a receipt for each payment, and a reminder before a renewal where the law or the plan requires one
- If a payment fails, I will tell you and try again, and you can update your details. If it still fails, I may pause or end the subscription after giving you notice

## Price changes

I may change the price of a plan. I will give you at least 30 days’ notice by email before any increase takes effect. The new price applies from your next renewal after the notice period. If you do not want to pay it, you can cancel before it takes effect and you will not be charged the new price.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, using the account or billing page, or by emailing {{site.contactEmail | mailto}}. I will not make cancelling harder than signing up.

- If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for, unless I have ended the subscription for a breach
- You will not be charged again after you cancel
- I do not usually refund the part of a billing period that has already started, unless the law, these terms or the plan page says otherwise

### Your right to cancel and be refunded (consumers)

If you are a consumer, you have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of signing up for a subscription to a service or digital content, and receive a refund. If you ask me to start the service or give you access to digital content within that period, you may have to pay for what you have used, and in some cases you lose the right to cancel once access has begun, provided you have given your express consent and acknowledgement. This is explained at checkout and in the [consumer rights section](/cancellation/#your-statutory-rights-as-a-consumer) of the booking and cancellation policy.

Your plan is personal to you, or to the organisation named on it, and you must not share your login or let others use it unless the plan allows more users. You must keep to the [Acceptable use policy](/acceptableuse/). If a plan has usage limits, such as API calls or storage, they are shown on the plan page and I will warn you before you reach them where possible. {{site.commercial.apiLimits}}

## Your content and data

You keep the rights in content and data you put into a service. You give me permission to store and process it to provide the service. Personal data is handled as set out in the [Privacy notice](/privacy/) and the [Data protection policy](/dataprotection/).

You can export your data from a service while your subscription is active, where the service allows it. After a subscription ends, I will keep your data for a short time so you can rejoin or export it, and then delete it, as set out in the [Data retention policy](/retention/).

## Service levels and support

Support is available through {{site.contactEmail | mailto}}. Paid services may have service levels set out in the [Service level agreement](/sla/). Otherwise, I aim to provide the service with reasonable care and skill but I do not promise that it will be uninterrupted or free of errors.

## Suspension and ending

I may suspend or end a subscription if:

- You break these terms or the [Acceptable use policy](/acceptableuse/), or put the service or others at risk
- A payment remains unpaid after reminders
- I need to stop offering the plan or the service. I will give you at least 30 days’ notice where possible, and refund any amount you have paid for the period after the plan ends

If I end your subscription for a reason that is not your fault, I will refund the unused part of what you have paid.

## Liability

Nothing in these terms limits or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Other limits on liability are set out in the [Conditions of sale](/conditionsofsale/).

## Problems and complaints

If something goes wrong, contact {{site.contactEmail | mailto}} and I will put it right where I can. You can also use the [Complaints policy](/complaints/).

## Changes to these terms

I may update these terms from time to time. If a change affects an existing subscription in a way that is worse for you, I will tell you in advance and you may cancel. The current version is published on this page with the date it was last updated.
