## Overview

This cookie notice explains how {{site.siteUrl | link}} uses cookies, local storage, pixels, scripts, device identifiers and related technologies. For simplicity, this notice refers to all of these as “cookies” unless a distinction matters.

Cookies can be set directly by this website, known as first-party cookies, or by another organisation whose service is used on the site, known as third-party cookies.

This notice should be read alongside the [privacy notice](/privacy/), which explains how personal information is handled more generally.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device, or read from it, when you visit. They can help a website remember information about your visit, such as a preference you have selected, whether you have seen a notice or how a feature should work.

Similar technologies can include local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, scripts, software-development kits, browser identifiers and device-level information. They can be used for similar purposes, including security, preferences, analytics, embedded content and advertising.

Cookies can be session cookies, which normally expire when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which remain for a specified period or until you delete them.

## How cookies are used

Depending on the features enabled on the site, cookies may be used for the following purposes:

- **Essential operation:** maintaining security, preventing abuse, remembering a cookie choice and delivering core website functionality.
- **Preferences:** remembering choices such as appearance, language, accessibility or display preferences where those features are available.
- **Analytics:** understanding how visitors find and use the site, identifying technical problems and improving content, navigation and performance.
- **Embedded content:** loading third-party features such as video, maps, social-media posts, forms, calendars or booking tools.
- **Marketing:** measuring advertising, building audiences, limiting repeated adverts or supporting remarketing, only if such tools are used and you make the relevant choice.

The actual technologies used are listed in the [cookie inventory](#cookie-inventory). This list must be kept up to date whenever a tool, embed, plugin, tag or service is added, removed or reconfigured.

## Browser controls

Most browsers allow you to view, delete or block cookies through their settings. You can also use private browsing modes, clear site data or configure browser extensions that limit tracking.

Browser controls can affect how websites work. Blocking all cookies may prevent parts of a website from functioning correctly, including sign-in, preference, security, booking or form features.

Browser settings do not necessarily replace the choices available through this website’s cookie-preference control. Where you withdraw consent, optional cookies should be prevented or removed in accordance with the configuration of the relevant consent platform.
