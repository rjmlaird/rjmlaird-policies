## Overview

This policy explains how long **{{site.controllerName}}** keeps personal data and other records, how they are reviewed, and how they are deleted or anonymised when they are no longer needed.

Keeping personal data for longer than necessary is a risk to the people it is about and a breach of the storage limitation principle in the UK GDPR. Deleting something I am legally required to keep is also a problem. This policy balances the two. It works with the [Data protection policy](/dataprotection/) and the [Privacy notice](/privacy/).

## Principles

- I keep personal data only as long as I need it for the purpose it was collected for, or as long as the law requires
- I set a period for each type of record and record the reason for it
- Retention periods are counted from the date given in the schedule below, for example the end of a service or the end of a financial year
- I delete data securely, or anonymise it so that a person can no longer be identified, when the period ends
- If a legal claim, complaint, investigation or safeguarding matter is open, I keep the relevant records until it is resolved, even if the normal period has ended

Where a record falls into more than one category, the longer period applies.

## Backups

Deleted data may remain in backups for a limited time before the backup is overwritten or expires. I do not restore deleted personal data from a backup except to recover from an incident, and if I do, I delete it again promptly. See the [Information security policy](/informationsecurity/).

## Deletion and anonymisation

When a retention period ends, I:

- Delete electronic records, including from the tools and cloud services where they were stored
- Securely destroy any paper records, for example by cross-cut shredding
- Anonymise data that I want to keep for statistics, so that no individual can be identified
- Ask providers who hold data for me to delete it, where I have a contract with them

I review stored records at least once a year to make sure the schedule is followed.

## Your requests

You can ask me to delete your personal data. I will do so unless I have a legal reason to keep it, for example tax records or safeguarding duties. If I cannot delete something, I will explain why and when it will be deleted. To make a request, contact {{site.privacyEmail | mailto}}. See your rights in the [Data protection policy](/dataprotection/).

## Responsibility and review

I am responsible for applying this policy. I review it at least once a year and whenever the law or my services change.
