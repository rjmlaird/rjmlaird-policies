## Who these conditions are for

- **Consumers** are people buying for purposes that are wholly or mainly outside their trade, business, craft or profession. Extra protections apply to you, and they are noted below.
- **Business customers** are organisations and individuals buying for business purposes.

If you are buying on behalf of an organisation, you confirm that you have authority to do so.

## Making a contract

Information on this website is an invitation to make an offer, not an offer in itself. A contract is made when I confirm your booking or order in writing or by email, or when you accept a proposal or quote that I have issued, whichever happens first.

Proposals and quotes are valid for the period stated on them, or for 30 days if none is stated, unless I withdraw them earlier.

I may decline an order, for example if a service is unavailable, the information you gave is incomplete, or I have reasonable grounds to think the order is fraudulent or unsuitable. If I decline after you have paid, I will refund you in full.

Delivery dates and times are estimates unless I have said they are fixed. For services, I will start and perform them within a reasonable time or by the date agreed.

If you are a consumer, the law says that:
