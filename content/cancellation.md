## Overview

This policy explains how to book, change or cancel calls, sessions, mentoring, tutoring, workshops, talks and other bookings with **{{site.controllerName}}**, and what happens if a session is missed or I have to cancel.

It applies together with the [terms of service](/termsofservice/) and the [payment and refund policy](/payments/). Tutoring is also covered by the [tutoring terms](/tutoring/). If you are a consumer, nothing in this policy limits your [statutory rights](#your-statutory-rights-as-a-consumer).

## Making a booking

Bookings are made through the booking portal at {{site.bookingUrl | link}}, by email, or by accepting a written proposal. A booking is confirmed when you receive a confirmation from me, or from the booking system on my behalf, and any payment or deposit due at booking has been received.

Please check the date, time, time zone, format and fee in your confirmation, and tell me straight away if anything is wrong. Sessions take place at the time stated in the confirmation, using the link, location or platform stated there.

## Rescheduling

You can reschedule a one-to-one session or call without charge if you give at least {{site.commercial.rescheduleNotice}} notice, using the link in your confirmation or by emailing {{site.contactEmail | mailto}}. I will do my best to offer an alternative time, but I cannot guarantee that one will be available.

Rescheduling inside that notice period is treated as a late cancellation unless I agree otherwise.

## Cancelling a booking

### One-to-one sessions and calls

If you cancel at least {{site.commercial.cancellationNotice}} before the start time, you will receive a full refund, or a credit if you prefer.

If you cancel later than that, {{site.commercial.lateCancellationCharge}}.

### Workshops, talks, events and group bookings

Workshops, talks, events and group bookings need more preparation and planning. If you cancel or reschedule at least {{site.commercial.groupCancellationNotice}} before the event, you will receive a refund of any fee paid, less any non-recoverable costs I have already incurred and told you about.

If you cancel later than that, {{site.commercial.groupLateCancellationCharge}}. Travel, accommodation and other booked costs that cannot be recovered remain payable.

### Ongoing engagements

Cancellation of an ongoing or retained engagement is covered by the ending-the-agreement terms in the [terms of service](/termsofservice/) and your proposal.

## Late arrivals and missed sessions

If you are late, the session will normally still end at its scheduled time. I will wait for {{site.commercial.lateArrivalWait}} after the start time. If you have not joined or contacted me by then, I may treat the session as missed.

If you miss a session without giving the notice above, {{site.commercial.noShowCharge}}. If you have a genuine emergency, please contact me as soon as you can, and I will consider whether to waive or reduce the charge.

## If I need to cancel or reschedule

Sometimes I have to cancel or move a booking, for example through illness, an emergency, technical failure or a conflict I could not avoid. If this happens I will tell you as soon as I can and offer a new date or time.

If a new date does not suit you, you can have a full refund of anything you have paid for the session that has not taken place. I will not charge you for a session I cancel.

## Technical problems

If an online session cannot go ahead because of a technical problem, I will try to fix it or move to another platform. If we cannot reasonably complete the session, I will offer to rearrange it or, if you prefer, refund the affected session. If the problem is on your side, the session may be treated as used, but I will use reasonable judgement and I may offer to rearrange it.

## Your statutory rights as a consumer

If you are a consumer and make your booking online, by phone or by email, you normally have the right to cancel within 14 days of the day the contract is made, without giving a reason.

If you want the service to start within that 14-day period, I will ask you to confirm that clearly. If you then cancel within the 14 days, you will pay for what has been provided up to the time you tell me you are cancelling, in proportion to the full service. If the service is fully carried out within the 14 days with your agreement, you may lose the right to cancel.

To cancel under this right, tell me clearly in writing, for example by emailing {{site.contactEmail | mailto}}. You can use any clear statement. I will refund payments due without undue delay and within 14 days of being told you are cancelling, using the same payment method.

## Business clients

If you are a business client, the cancellation terms above and in your proposal apply, and there is no statutory cooling-off period. A proposal may set different terms for larger or bespoke work, and where it does, the proposal applies.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method and follow the process in the [payment and refund policy](/payments/).

## Questions and complaints

If you are unsure how this policy applies to your booking, contact {{site.contactEmail | mailto}} before you cancel. If you are unhappy with how a booking or cancellation was handled, see the [complaints policy](/complaints/).

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy as my services change. The version that applies to a booking is the one in force when you made it.
