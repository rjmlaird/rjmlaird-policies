## About these terms

These terms apply to one-to-one and group tutoring, mentoring and similar learning support provided by **{{site.controllerName}}**, in person or online. They are in addition to the [terms of service](/termsofservice/), the [booking and cancellation policy](/cancellation/) and the [payment and refund policy](/payments/).

In these terms, a **learner** is the person receiving tuition. Where the learner is under 18, the contract is with their **parent or carer**, who is responsible for these terms and for payment.

## What tutoring involves

Tutoring is tailored support to help a learner understand a subject, build skills and prepare for study or assessment. Subjects, levels, session length and frequency are agreed when you book.

Tutoring is not a replacement for school or college teaching, pastoral support, special educational needs provision or professional advice, and the tutor is not a substitute for a learner’s teachers or carers.

## Learners under 18

Tutoring for learners under 18 is arranged with a parent or carer, who must agree to these terms and the [safeguarding policy](/safeguarding/) before sessions begin.

Parents and carers should:

- Provide accurate contact details and tell me about anything that may affect a learner’s safety, wellbeing or ability to take part, such as access needs or things that the learner may find difficult
- Make sure a responsible adult can be contacted during sessions, and know when and where an online session is taking place
- Provide a suitable, quiet space and working equipment for online sessions
- Pay fees and give notice of changes as set out in the cancellation and payment policies

## Sessions

Sessions start and end at the agreed times. I will prepare for each session and aim to explain concepts clearly, set suitable work, and give feedback.

Learners are expected to attend on time, take part, complete agreed work between sessions where set, and bring any materials needed. Progress depends on regular attendance and practice.

## Online sessions

Online sessions take place on an agreed video platform. I will send the link in advance. Learners should join from a suitable shared or open area of the home where possible, and use a suitable account, name and background.

Sessions are not recorded unless this has been agreed in advance in line with the [photography and media consent policy](/mediaconsent/). Learners and parents or carers must not record, screenshot or share sessions without agreement.

## Conduct

I expect learners, parents and carers to treat each other respectfully. I will treat learners with respect and professionalism, and follow the safeguarding policy. Bullying, harassment, discrimination, abusive language, inappropriate content or inappropriate contact will not be tolerated, and I may pause or end tutoring if it happens.

Contact with learners takes place through agreed channels, normally with a parent or carer included in messages. I do not accept social media connections from learners.

## Attendance, rescheduling and cancellation

Rescheduling, cancellation, lateness and missed sessions are covered in the [booking and cancellation policy](/cancellation/). Sessions that are cancelled or missed inside the notice period may be charged.

If a learner is unwell, please tell me as early as possible, and I will try to rearrange.

## Fees and payment

Fees are agreed when you book and are payable as set out in the [payment and refund policy](/payments/). For learners under 18, the parent or carer is responsible for payment.

## Materials and intellectual property

Worksheets, notes, presentations and other materials I create are for the learner’s own study. They must not be shared, copied or published without my permission. See the [terms of use](/termsofuse/) for rules on reusing website content.

I will not ask a learner to submit another person’s work, and I will not do a learner’s assessed work for them. Where tutoring supports coursework or assessed work, I help the learner to understand and improve, and the work submitted must be the learner’s own.

## No guarantee of results

I will provide tutoring with reasonable care and skill, but I cannot guarantee a particular grade, examination result, admission, scholarship, job or other outcome. Results depend on many factors, including the learner’s effort and the requirements of the examining or admitting body, which can change. Check current requirements with the relevant school, board or institution. See also the [website disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

## Safeguarding and wellbeing

If I am concerned about the safety or wellbeing of a learner, I will follow the [safeguarding policy](/safeguarding/). This may mean sharing information with the parent or carer, or with children’s services or the police, where necessary to keep the learner safe.

## Personal information

I handle personal information about learners, parents and carers as set out in the [privacy notice](/privacy/). I collect only what I need to arrange and deliver tutoring and to keep learners safe, and I keep it only for as long as needed.

## Ending tutoring

You can stop tutoring at any time by telling me, subject to the notice and cancellation terms. I may end tutoring if these terms are not followed, if I cannot continue for good reason, or where I believe it is in the learner’s best interests. I will give notice where I can and refund any fees paid for sessions that have not taken place.

## Complaints

If you have a concern about tutoring, please tell me first. You can also use the [complaints policy](/complaints/).

## Changes to these terms

I may update these terms from time to time. The version that applies is the one in force when you booked, unless the law requires otherwise.
