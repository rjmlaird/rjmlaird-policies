## Overview

I aim to provide a good service and to deal with problems fairly and promptly. If something has gone wrong, I want to hear about it and put it right where I can.

This policy covers complaints about this website, enquiries, bookings, payments, tutoring, talks, advisory work and other services provided by **{{site.controllerName}}**, and about my conduct. It does not limit any legal rights you have.

## What this policy covers

You can use this policy to complain about:

- The standard of a service or how a booking was handled
- A fee, invoice or refund decision
- The behaviour or conduct of anyone acting for me
- The accuracy or content of something published on this website
- How this website, a form or a booking process worked

Some matters have their own route:

- **Safeguarding concerns** about a child or adult at risk: follow the [safeguarding policy](/safeguarding/). If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.
- **Privacy and data-protection concerns:** contact {{site.privacyEmail | mailto}} and see the [privacy notice](/privacy/).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** follow the [security notice](/security/).
- **Accessibility barriers:** see the [accessibility statement](/accessibility/).

## How to make a complaint

Email {{site.contactEmail | mailto}} with “Complaint” in the subject line. If you would prefer to use another route, use the [contact page](/contact/).

It helps if you include:

- Your name and contact details, and the name of the learner if the complaint concerns a child
- What happened, when it happened and who was involved
- Any booking reference, invoice number or relevant correspondence
- What you would like me to do to put it right

You can ask someone to complain on your behalf, and I will tell you if I need your written permission to deal with them. If you need to complain in a different format, tell me and I will make reasonable adjustments.

## How complaints are handled

1. **Acknowledgement:** I will acknowledge your complaint within {{site.complaints.acknowledgementDays}} business days.
2. **Investigation:** I will look into what happened, review the relevant records and may contact you for more information.
3. **Response:** I will reply in writing within {{site.complaints.responseDays}} business days, explaining what I found, what I will do and why. If an investigation will take longer, I will tell you why and when to expect an answer.
4. **Review:** If you are not satisfied, you can ask for the decision to be reviewed. I will look again at the complaint and the response, and write to you with the outcome.

I will treat complaints confidentially and share information only where needed to investigate or where I am required to by law.

## Outcomes

Depending on the issue, an outcome may include an explanation, an apology, correcting information, repeating or rescheduling a service, a full or partial refund under the [payment and refund policy](/payments/), or changes to how I work. Making a complaint will not affect how I treat you.

## Complaints about me

If your complaint is about me personally, I will still consider it fairly and will keep a written record of how I have handled it. If you do not think I can deal with it fairly, you can raise it with an appropriate external body, listed below.

## Taking things further

If you are not satisfied with the outcome:

- For data-protection matters, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner’s Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
- For safeguarding matters, you can contact the local authority children’s services or the police, and the NSPCC helpline on 0808 800 5000.
- {{site.complaints.adrStatement}}
- You may also be able to take legal advice or bring a claim in court. The Citizens Advice consumer service can explain your rights if you are a consumer.

## Records and improvement

I keep a record of complaints, outcomes and any changes made as a result, so that I can learn from them. Complaint records are kept in line with the retention periods in the [privacy notice](/privacy/).

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as my services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with the date it was last updated.
