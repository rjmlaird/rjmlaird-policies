## Our commitment

I aim to make this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies, navigate by keyboard, use magnification or require reduced motion. This includes people who are disabled or neurodivergent, have temporary impairments, or use different devices and browsers.

I am continually improving the accessibility of this site and consider accessibility throughout content, design and development work. This includes using semantic HTML, clear language, accessible interaction patterns and testing with a combination of automated and manual methods.

If something does not work for you, or you need information in another format, please get in touch using the [feedback and contact](#feedback-and-contact) details.

## Conformance status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for improving the accessibility of web content. WCAG 2.2 defines three conformance levels: A, AA and AAA.

**This website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA.**

Partially conformant means that some parts of the website may not fully conform to the applicable accessibility standard. This status reflects the current state of testing and does not mean that every page or interaction has been assessed comprehensively.

I will update this statement when significant changes are made, when new testing identifies an issue or when known issues have been resolved. A conformance claim should reflect recent testing and should identify known limitations clearly.

## Measures supporting accessibility

I take the following measures to support accessibility:

- Include accessibility requirements in planning, design and development.
- Use semantic HTML and meaningful document structure.
- Review accessibility during code review and content review.
- Test keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
- Test interactive components with assistive technologies where practical.
- Use automated testing to identify potential accessibility issues.
- Track known issues and improvements in the relevant project repository.
- Welcome accessibility feedback and use it to improve the site.
- Consider accessibility when selecting third-party services, embeds and media.

Accessibility is an ongoing responsibility rather than a one-time compliance exercise. Automated tools can identify some problems, but they cannot determine whether every interaction, explanation or piece of content works well for every person. I also aim to use clear language, explain specialist terms where useful and structure long content into manageable sections.

## Accessibility features

The website is designed to include the following features where relevant:

- A skip link to bypass repeated navigation and move directly to the main content.
- Semantic HTML elements, including header, nav, main, section and footer.
- A logical heading hierarchy.
- Descriptive page titles and link text.
- Keyboard access to interactive elements.
- Visible focus indicators.
- Appropriate landmark regions for assistive technology navigation.
- Responsive layouts for different screen sizes and orientations.
- Support for user preferences such as reduced motion where applicable.
- Text alternatives for informative images.
- Decorative images marked so they are not unnecessarily announced.
- Accessible labels and descriptions for form controls.
- Clear identification of form errors and required fields.
- Appropriate language attributes to support pronunciation.
- Accessible colour contrast for text and essential interface elements.
- Content that remains usable when text is enlarged.
- Accessible handling of menus, disclosures, tabs and other interactive components where present.
- Screen-reader labels for controls whose purpose is not clear from visible text.
- Links to transcripts, captions or alternative formats where those are available.

Not every feature is present on every page. Some content may depend on third-party services or embedded technologies whose accessibility I cannot fully control.

## Keyboard and assistive technology support

The website is intended to support keyboard-only navigation without requiring a mouse or other pointing device, and to work with current mainstream browsers and assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, browser zoom, reading tools and voice-control software.

Where interactive components are used, I aim to provide:

- Logical focus order.
- Visible focus styles.
- Keyboard-operable controls.
- Appropriate names, roles and states.
- Sensible focus management when menus, dialogs or other temporary interfaces open and close.
- Escape-key support where it is appropriate for dismissible interfaces.

Support may vary according to the browser, operating system, assistive technology and device being used. The website does not require JavaScript for basic page reading and navigation wherever that can reasonably be avoided, although some enhanced interactions may depend on JavaScript.

If you experience a problem with a particular browser, device or assistive technology, please include those details when reporting it. This makes the problem easier to reproduce.

## Content and formats

If you cannot access something on this website because of its format, content, design or technical implementation, you can ask for help or an alternative version. Depending on the material, I may be able to provide:

- A plain-text or HTML version of information.
- A text-only summary of a page, article, image, diagram or chart.
- A larger-print, higher-contrast or simplified version of content.
- A structured text version of a downloadable document.
- A data table or written explanation for a chart or visualisation.
- A transcript, summary or alternative route for audio or video content.
- An alternative way to make an enquiry, arrange a conversation or access a service.

Please describe what you are trying to access and the format that would be most useful. This helps me respond more effectively.

## Technical information

The accessibility of this website relies on the following technologies:

- HTML.
- CSS.
- JavaScript, where needed for enhanced interactions.
- WAI-ARIA, used only where native HTML does not provide the required semantics.
- SVG, images, audio, video and other media formats where used.
- Modern web browsers and assistive technologies.

The site is built with Astro. The technology used to generate a page does not itself guarantee accessibility; the accessibility of the resulting content and interface is what matters.

WCAG conformance depends on using technologies in an accessibility-supported way, and on making information or functionality available through an accessible method where a particular technology is not accessible.

## Assessment approach

I assess accessibility through a combination of:

- Self-evaluation by the site maintainer.
- Automated testing with tools such as axe, Lighthouse and WAVE.
- Keyboard-only navigation.
- Browser zoom and text enlargement checks.
- Responsive testing across different viewport sizes.
- Manual inspection of headings, landmarks, labels, links and focus order.
- Screen-reader testing with tools such as VoiceOver, NVDA or other available assistive technologies.
- Review of colour contrast and non-text contrast.
- Testing of forms, menus, dialogs, tabs and other interactive components.
- Community feedback and accessibility audits where available.

Automated testing is used as an aid rather than as proof of full accessibility. W3C recommends that an accessibility statement include practical information about known limitations, testing environments and the ways accessibility has been assessed.

## Escalating a concern

This is an independent professional website and is not presented as a public-sector website or mobile application for the purposes of the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018.

If you have raised an accessibility concern and do not believe it has been addressed appropriately, please contact me again with the details and the support you need. I will review the matter and try to find a reasonable solution.

You may also wish to seek independent advice about your rights under applicable equality, consumer, education or other relevant law. This statement does not limit any rights you may have under law.
