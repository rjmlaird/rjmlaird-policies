## Overview

This policy explains when photographs, video and audio recordings may be made at or from workshops, talks, tutoring sessions, events and other activities run by **{{site.controllerName}}**, how they may be used, and how to give, refuse or withdraw consent.

Images and recordings of people can be personal data. This policy works together with the [privacy notice](/privacy/) and the [safeguarding policy](/safeguarding/).

## Principles

- Images and recordings are made only where there is a clear purpose.
- You are told before anything is recorded, and you can say no.
- Saying no will not affect a booking or the service you receive, unless recording is essential to the service and I have made that clear beforehand.
- Images and recordings of children and young people are treated with particular care.

## What this covers

- Photographs and video of participants, speakers and audiences at workshops, talks and events
- Audio and video recordings of online sessions, talks and webinars
- Testimonials and quotations provided by you, with or without a photograph
- Screenshots or other captures of online sessions

## Tutoring and one-to-one sessions

One-to-one tutoring and mentoring sessions are not recorded unless this has been agreed in advance in writing. Where a session is recorded, it is used for the agreed purpose only, for example for the learner to review a session, and is not published or shared without separate consent.

Learners, parents and carers must not record, screenshot or share sessions without agreement.

## Workshops, talks and events

For events run by me, I will tell attendees in advance if photography or recording will take place, and where possible I will mark areas or provide a way to avoid being photographed or recorded, such as sitting in a designated area. If an organiser or venue is responsible for recording, that organiser’s notice also applies.

I will not photograph or record a child or young person without the consent of a parent or carer.

## How images and recordings may be used

Subject to your consent, images and recordings may be used for:

- Showing what my work involves, on this website and my professional profiles
- Promotional and marketing material
- Reports, funding or evaluation, and case studies
- Internal training, review and quality purposes
- {{site.controllerName}}’s agreed learning activities, such as a recording given to participants

I will not use images or recordings in a way that could embarrass or harm you or misrepresent you. I will not publish a child’s full name alongside their image.

## Consent

Where consent is needed, I will ask for it clearly and separately from other terms, explaining what will be recorded, why, where it may be published and for how long. Consent can be given in writing, through a booking-form tick box or by another clear action that I can record.

For people under 18, consent is given by a parent or carer. Where appropriate, I will also ask the young person for their views.

You do not have to consent, and you can choose to consent to some uses only.

## Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing {{site.privacyEmail | mailto}}. I will stop using the material for future purposes and remove it from places I control within a reasonable time. I cannot recall printed material already distributed, or remove material that others have already copied or shared outside my control, and I will tell you if this applies.

Withdrawing consent does not affect use that took place before you withdrew.

## Storage, security and retention

Images and recordings are stored securely, with access limited to those who need it, and are not kept for longer than needed for the purpose. Retention periods are described in the [privacy notice](/privacy/).

## Testimonials

I will ask for your permission before publishing a testimonial or quotation, and will tell you how your name, role or organisation will be shown. You can ask me to edit or remove a testimonial.

## Images taken by others

At events, other attendees may take photographs. Where an event is run by me, I will ask people to respect others’ wishes and not to publish images of children other than their own. I cannot control what others do, but I will respond to any concern.

## Questions and concerns

If you have a question or concern about an image or recording, contact {{site.privacyEmail | mailto}}. If you want to complain, see the [complaints policy](/complaints/).
