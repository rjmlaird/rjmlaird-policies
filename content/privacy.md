## Information collected

The information collected depends on how you use the site and the context of your interaction. It may include the following categories.

### Contact and identity information

- Name
- Email address
- Telephone number, where you provide it
- Organisation, role, sector and professional profile information
- Preferred contact method and communication preferences

### Enquiry and project information

- Your message and the reason you are contacting me
- Project, service, event or collaboration context
- Relevant links, documents or attachments you choose to share
- Timescales, budget information and intended outcomes, where provided
- Meeting notes, correspondence and agreed next steps

### Speaking, media and public-engagement information

- Event, programme, publication or project name
- Audience, subject, brief, format and session requirements
- Date, location, online platform, travel and accessibility information
- Recording, photography, editorial, licensing or usage requirements

### Technical and usage information

- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Browser, device, operating system and screen information
- Pages viewed, referring source, site interactions and error information
- Security, performance and service-operation logs
- Cookie identifiers and consent preferences, where applicable

### Financial and transaction information

If work is agreed, relevant records may include invoices, payment status, tax information and associated correspondence. Payment-card details are handled by the relevant payment provider and are not stored directly by me unless explicitly stated otherwise.

### Information you choose to provide

You may choose to provide additional information through forms, email, documents, calls or other communications. Please provide only what is relevant to your enquiry or the work being discussed.

Most personal information is collected directly from you, for example when you:

- Submit a form, send an email or arrange a call
- Subscribe to optional updates
- Provide information during a project, event or collaboration
- Use the website and its essential or consented technologies

In limited circumstances, information may also come from:

- A colleague, employer, client, parent, guardian or event organiser acting on your behalf
- Publicly available professional sources, such as your organisation’s website or public professional profile
- A referral partner, collaborator or associated organisation, where relevant to a legitimate enquiry
- Service providers that support website, booking, payment or communication processes

If I receive your information from someone else in a way that requires further privacy information to be provided, I will do so as required by applicable law.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

These measures may include:

- Access controls and least-privilege access to relevant systems
- Strong account security and multi-factor authentication where available
- Encrypted connections where supported by the service provider
- Reputable hosting, payment, booking and communication providers
- Regular review of systems, suppliers and access arrangements
- Appropriate procedures for responding to suspected data incidents

No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary email or website forms.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and the legal basis used, you may have the right to:

- Ask for access to the personal information held about you
- Ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be corrected
- Ask for information to be erased in certain circumstances
- Ask for processing to be restricted in certain circumstances
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Receive a portable copy of certain information you provided
- Withdraw consent at any time where consent is relied upon
- Complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office

### How to make a request

To exercise a right, email {{site.privacyEmail | mailto}}. Please state what you are requesting and provide enough information to help identify the relevant records.

I may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Requests are normally handled without charge, although a reasonable fee or refusal may be permitted where a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.
